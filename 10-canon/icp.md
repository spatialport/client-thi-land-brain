# Icp

Purpose: Who the business serves, jobs, pains, buying context, objections, geography and evidence.

## Accepted knowledge

- Primary persona: parents aged 35–50 (ADV targeting notes also say 28–45), predominantly mums as decision-makers (~60/40 mums/dads for the first visit), transversal professions, Swiss family income (~CHF 7,000/month average salary) making memberships sustainable; they want safe, clean, practical fun for their child after school (school ends 15:30) (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md; MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md).
- End users are children 3–10, segmented: 3–6 years → soft play, slides, ball pits, protected areas (communication: parental reassurance); 7–10 years → physical-interactive arcade, air hockey, prize games, table football (communication: fun, challenge, points and prizes) (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Double addressee rule: every message speaks to two people at once — the child who dreams of the adventure and the parent who decides and pays (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md).
- Geography: primary catchment 5 min (Lumino, Castione, Arbedo, Claro, Gnosca, Gorduno, San Vittore, Rovereto — weekday clientele); secondary 10–15 min (Contone, all Bellinzonese, part of Grigioni up to Biasca — weekend); tertiary weekend (Lugano and beyond). Daniele cites 80,000 families within 20 km, ~7% with children in target. Cross-border commuters not a priority; target is resident Ticinesi (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Accessibility proof points: Bellinzona Nord motorway exit 1 km, Castione train station 1 km, AutoPostale bus stop 50–60 m from the centre, shared Centro Opti parking (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Strong latent demand: drop-off (leaving the child without the parent) emerged as a very strong request from Lumino mums (~12-mum WhatsApp group); would be a huge differentiator but needs qualified staff and insurance (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- B2B segment: after-school centres ("Il Girasole", contact Lisa already started), schools, doposcuola, nurseries, parents' associations, paediatricians — group-visit agreements; birthday/team-building with families to explore (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Secondary segments from the value-proposition matrix: multi-child families (THI FAMILY), early-adopter "fans of the project" (Founder), families with an upcoming birthday, tourists/rainy-day families in Ticino (Google "cosa fare con bambini Ticino") (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md).
- Where the target gets information: 1) Instagram (main channel for mums 35–50), 2) Facebook, 3) Google Search ("parco giochi bambini Bellinzona"), 4) word of mouth in the local community, 5) school parents' WhatsApp groups (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Objections/stakes: cleanliness is a huge reputational risk; the Founder purchase pre-opening is "un atto di fede" (buying a place not yet seen) so communication must compensate (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
