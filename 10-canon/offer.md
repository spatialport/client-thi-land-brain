# Offer

Purpose: Products/services, economics, proof, differentiation and current commercial priorities.

## Accepted knowledge

**Entry price list — definitive, from the Business Plan (ev-tl-004, dec-tl-014):** child **CHF 10**, accompanying adult **CHF 3**, under 2 free. Anti-slip socks CHF 5, mandatory to play, sold separately. Planning assumption: 1 paying adult per child, so average revenue per family unit CHF 13. This supersedes both the earlier "CHF 12 child / CHF 3–4 adult" figures and the "CHF 10 per person flat" of the 5 Aug 2026 marketing changelog — the latter is what several published surfaces still say and must be corrected.

Preview/Open Day ticket CHF 7 per child, entry + anti-slip socks included (separate value CHF 15, saving CHF 8); accompanying adults free at the preview only (dec-tl-008, pending Daniele's confirmation). No T-shirt included in any offer.

Memberships (Business Plan, ev-tl-004; supersedes MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md §6 on naming):
- THI **STANDARD** CHF 49/month (6 entries) · THI PLUS CHF 69/month (10 entries) · THI FAMILY CHF 99/month (8 entries, 2 children) · THI **FOUNDER CHF 350 one-off** (una tantum, not recurring — dec-tl-016; 8 entries/month, the adult pays normally).
- The entry tier is **STANDARD, not START**. Allegato A lists four digital cards as START/PLUS/FAMILY/FOUNDER: verify the produced artwork (task ev-tl-004-t1).
- Founder modelled at 30/60/90 sign-ups in year one (CHF 10,500 / 21,000 / 31,500), booked as year-one cash and kept out of recurring revenue.
- Founder stays **off the landing until the 31 October opening** (dec-tl-017): the preview funnel sells the ticket only.
- Sales strategy: pre-launch only the Founder tier is sold; other tiers are proposed after the first visit, once the customer has experienced the value (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md). Cards are digital (wallet) except the Founder physical card (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).

Founder campaign (waves, MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md §6; 00-OVERVIEW-MARKETING-PLAN.md):
- Wave 1 = first 100 → membership until the child turns 10 at CHF 350/year one-off framing, plus perks (name on the Founder wall/cartellone, physical card, event priority); realistic target ~70, closed live at the Open Day.
- Wave 2 = next 100 → 20% off first year (Nov–Dec 2026) · Wave 3 = next 100 → 15% off (from Jan 2027) · first 15 days after opening → 10% off.
- Mandatory 4–5 question survey at Founder registration (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).

Open Day / preview: 300 children total, 2-hour slots, max 30 children per slot, Sat 24 + Sun 25 October 2026; ticket CHF 7; Founder pitch delivered live during the event; ticket revenue 300 × CHF 7 = CHF 2,100 (00-OVERVIEW-MARKETING-PLAN.md).

**Birthday packages — definitive (ev-tl-004, dec-tl-015).** The main acquisition engine: each party brings 10–15 new children. Naming is settled — neither the contract's Bronze/Platino/Gold nor the interim Base/Base con torta/Completo survive.

| Package | Price | Children | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| THI PARTY | CHF 18/child | min 10, max 15 | entry + table + games + popcorn + crisps + drink |
| THI PARTY PLUS | CHF 23/child | min 10, max 18 | THI PARTY + pizza |
| THI EXCLUSIVE PARTY | CHF 499 flat | up to 20 | PLUS + exclusive use max 3h + socks + mascot at the cake |
| EXCLUSIVE extra | CHF 18/child | 21st–25th | surcharge beyond 20 children |

- **Anti-slip socks are included in all three packages** (dec-tl-015), not only EXCLUSIVE as the model states. Nothing to bring.
- Contribution margin ~72–73% across all three — the highest-margin line in the business. Variable cost assumptions: CHF 4/child PARTY, CHF 8/child PLUS, CHF 120 base EXCLUSIVE, CHF 8/child extra.
- Volumes modelled at 4/6/8 PARTY, 2/4/6 PLUS, 1/2/3 EXCLUSIVE per month → CHF 21,252 / 44,088 / 72,252 per year.
- Managed by Verena; autonomous online booking (Booking-style real-time calendar); extras: themed cake (external bakery), photographer, THI mascot appearance, treasure hunt, pizza/buffet from the restaurant (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Still open: maximum parties per day and bookable slots (task ev-tl-004-t4); partner bakery name.

Other revenue and offer elements:
- Arcade area: physical interactive games (hit-the-ball, water shooter, bowling, prize games — no passive video games), supplied/maintained by an external partner at no upfront cost; monetized with CHF 1 coins, card also accepted (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Ticket-redemption systems are illegal in Switzerland (minor gambling incitement); the loyalty points system is deliberately decoupled from arcade winnings (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Points system: earned via entries, birthdays, premium services, activities; spent on exclusive prizes (mascot plushies, gadgets) or converted to entries/discounts; max ~1,000 points unlockable per month (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Merchandise: t-shirts, caps, keyrings purchasable; mascot plushies and special prizes only redeemable with points; merch sample quality control tracked as a task (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md; projects.ts/tasks "Merch: controllo qualità campioni").
- Annual events: Easter, Halloween, Christmas, inauguration — priced, with member priority (MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md; THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Restaurant synergy: 10% discount at THI Kitchen/Restaurant for park-wristband holders; food not included in memberships; Saturday "giro pizza" (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md; MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md).
- **Opening hours — definitive, seven days a week (ev-tl-004, dec-tl-018):** Mon/Tue/Thu **15:00–19:00** · Wed **09:00–19:00** (split: 09–13 covered by hourly support, 13–19 two staff) · Fri **15:00–22:00** · Sat **09:00–22:00** (THI Night) · Sun **09:00–19:00**. These supersede the MASTER_PROMPT §6 hours (Mon/Tue/Thu 15–20 · Wed 9–20 · Fri 15–21:30 · Sat 9–22:30 · Sun 9–20): every day closes earlier except Friday, which extends to 22:00.
- Revenue ambition: target revenue ~CHF 56k with ROI ~19× on the launch ADV plan (00-OVERVIEW-MARKETING-PLAN.md, client-defined Open Day digital strategy).

## Economics (internal only — never in client-facing deliverables)

From the Business Plan, ev-tl-004:
- Cost base: staff CHF 124,755/year (2 employees at 80% of CHF 4,500 gross + 17 h/week support at CHF 25/h, incl. 15% employer charges) · rent CHF 54,000 · other operating costs CHF 36,000 · initial equipment investment CHF 50,000 (input still to confirm).
- **Break-even: 45.4 children per day** on entries alone.
- Scenarios — prudent 30 children/day → recurring EBITDA **−CHF 40,793**, year-1 cash −80,293 · central 50/day → +71,005, cash +42,005 · positive 75/day → +209,948, cash +191,448.
- The prudent scenario loses money. The distance between prudent and viable is roughly **15 additional children per day** — that gap is what marketing has to close, and it is the right lens for the open ADV budget question (task ev-tl-003-t3).
