## Proposed knowledge (awaiting review by Alex)

### Brand truth
- Purpose: give Ticino families an indoor play space where children have safe, active fun and parents genuinely feel good — the "boutique" opposite of the chaotic "capannone" (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md; 02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md).
- Claim: «Dove l'energia diventa gioia.» — official, from the brandbook; every plan document closes with it (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md; 00-OVERVIEW-MARKETING-PLAN.md).
- Vision: transform the concept of indoor play in Ticino. Mission: create the reference point for families of the territory (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md, citing the brandbook).
- Reasons to believe: age-separated areas, physical-interactive games (no screens), toilets checked every 30–60 min, ~100-person capacity cap, low-noise construction, Centro Opti ecosystem (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md).

### Visual identity (tokens from brandbook via MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md §5 — verify against NXTO/THI-LAND-BRAND-BOOK.html)
- Colors: Teal #006A61 (primary) · Lime #B2F746 (vivid accent) · Dark #191C1E (text) · Mint #E0F5F2 (light backgrounds) · Orange #FF5500 (warm/energy accent) · Amber #FBBF24 (joy accent) · White. Vivid, joyful palette.
- Typography: Arton (Regular + Slant) ONLY for the "THI LAND" logotype — never headings/body. Outfit (Google Fonts) for everything else: headings 700/800, body 300–600. Arton is a proprietary font stored in NXTO/clienti/THI-LAND/ (Arton-*.otf).
- Style: rounded forms, energy, colour, safe fun — the visual opposite of luxury minimalism (MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md).
- Logo assets: LOGO-1.svg / LOGO-2.svg / HEAD.svg in NXTO/clienti/THI-LAND/; thi-logo.svg and okkio-head.svg in projects/thi-land/presentazioni/assets/ (MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md; workspace audit).

### Mascot
- The mascot is the face of the brand: a rounded teal cyclops with soft organic curves, named OKKIO in MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md §5 ("Mascotte: OKKIO, un ciclope tondeggiante color teal"); earlier and marketing documents call it "mascotte THI" (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md; contract Phase 4 "Chatbot mascotte THI"). Naming conflict — see Open questions.
- Personality: "il bimbo che parla col bimbo" — friendly, reassuring, playful; speaks primarily to children; used in illustrations, chatbot voice, plush merchandise (points-only prizes), physical costume for events, 3D wall mural (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md; THI_LAND_Contratto_testo.txt Allegato A).

### Voice
- Sounds like: playful, warm, energetic, joyful; second person ("tu", "il tuo bambino"); action/adventure verbs (scivola, lanciati, scopri, vinci, ridi); concrete reassurance on safety and cleanliness; measured emoji on social (🎈🛝🎉), never in documents (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md §7).
- Never sounds like: cold/institutional ("la gentile clientela"), bureaucratic ("fruizione del servizio ludico", "l'utente", "il minore"), vague promises, emoji spam, saccharine baby-talk to parents, needless anglicisms (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md §7).
- Golden rule: dual addressee always — one line for the child (imagination, adventure, mascot), one for the parent (safety, hygiene, value, membership convenience) (MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md; 02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md).
- Register: "premium ma accessibile" — not luxury, not cheap (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).

### Languages and compliance
- Internal strategy documents in Italian; public-facing materials IT (default) + DE + EN (MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md).
- Minors' privacy is a brand constraint: photos of children only with signed release, no child profiling, parental consent for data, nLPD (Swiss) + GDPR (MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md; 00-OVERVIEW-MARKETING-PLAN.md).
