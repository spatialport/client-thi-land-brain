# Positioning

Purpose: Unique advantage, category, alternatives, promises, reasons to believe and prohibited claims.

## Proposed knowledge (awaiting review by Alex)

- Positioning statement (source of truth, 02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md): "Per le famiglie con bambini dai 3 ai 10 anni del Bellinzonese e del Ticino, THI LAND è il parco gioco indoor del Centro Opti che unisce un'avventura sicura e divertente per i bambini a un ambiente curato e accogliente per i genitori, perché — a differenza dei grandi parchi-capannone — mettiamo la stessa cura nel divertimento dei bambini e nel benessere di chi li accompagna."
- Official claim (brandbook): «Dove l'energia diventa gioia.» Internal shorthand: "Il parco indoor 'boutique' delle famiglie ticinesi — pulito, sicuro, fatto bene." Vision: transform the concept of indoor play in Ticino. Mission: create the reference point for local families (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md).
- Category: premium-proximity indoor play park; positioned as the "boutique" alternative to big industrial parks — slightly higher price, markedly higher environment quality, capped capacity (~100 people) so never chaotic, integrated restaurant, community focus (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Four message pillars, always dual-addressee (child + parent) (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md):
  1. Avventura sicura per i bambini — age-separated areas, certified structures, toilets checked every 30–60 min, mascot as adventure companion.
  2. Valore e serenità per i genitori — quiet zones, restaurant below (10% wristband discount), memberships that cut cost-per-visit.
  3. L'unico parco indoor di qualità della zona — proximity proof points (motorway 1 km, station 1 km, bus 60 m), ~100-person cap, wood/plasterboard construction for low noise.
  4. Il cuore family del Centro Opti — play, food and health in one place; non-replicable ecosystem advantage.
- Named competitors (internal only): Piedone (anti-benchmark: 700–1,200 people/day, cheap, "capannone", 50 min from Lumino), Swiss Family Center Rohrbach (positive benchmark), Kiddy Dome (reference) (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Narrative enemy is the chaotic, neglected "parco-capannone", never a named competitor. Engagement rule: never name competitors in public materials; differentiation always implicit; never denigrate («alziamo noi l'asticella, non abbassiamo gli altri») (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md).
- Prohibited/avoid: vague promises ("massima qualità", "divertimento assicurato"), bureaucratic language ("fruizione del servizio ludico", "l'utente", "il minore"), needless anglicisms ("kids experience"), cold institutional tone; differentiate on care/safety/environment, not on price (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md).
- Elevator pitch and 50/100-word boilerplates exist ready-made in 02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md §4–5 (both close with the claim).
- Commercial objectives the positioning must serve: memberships (Founder first), birthdays as viral acquisition engine, recurring footfall (MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md; 00-OVERVIEW-MARKETING-PLAN.md).
