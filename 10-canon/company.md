# Company

Purpose: Who the company is, legal/operating context, history, beliefs, mistakes, lessons and five-year ambition.

## Proposed knowledge (awaiting review by Alex)

- THI LAND is an organized indoor play park ("spazio gioco organizzato") for children aged 3–10, located inside the Centro Opti in Lumino, Canton Ticino, Switzerland (THI_LAND_Contratto_testo.txt; THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- It is explicitly not a generic indoor playground: the ambition is a "boutique" — clean, curated, safe, pleasant for parents as well as children — as opposed to the big industrial "capannone" parks (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md: «Può essere come una "boutique" rispetto ai grandi parchi industriali»).
- Legal entity operating the project (per the OKKIO software contract): KRISTAL SA, UID CHE-115.363.630, represented by Daniele Pronzini (THI-LAND-OKKIO-CONTRATTO.md). The main services contract SP-2026-THI-001 left the client company fields blank (THI_LAND_Contratto_testo.txt).
- Origin story: Daniele and Verena, parents of Thiago, visited play parks in Italy and Switzerland and saw a gap in the Ticino market — no quality indoor play spaces near Lumino where the child has safe fun and the parent feels at ease. The name "THI" comes from Thiago; the brand is a tribute to the child who inspired the project (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Core values from discovery: safety (parents can leave the child with total peace of mind), cleanliness (toilets checked every 30–60 min, immaculate environment), active fun (physical interactive games, no passive video games/screens), parental comfort (noise-separated zones, coffee, socializing), community (a meeting point for local families, not just a service) (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Anti-model: "Piedone"-style loud, chaotic, cheap big parks; a place the parent wants to escape after an hour; passive screens; anything perceived as dirty or neglected (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Centro Opti ecosystem: the same complex hosts THI Kitchen / THI Restaurant (a canteen-style restaurant on the lower floor, run by Fabio) and Farmacia Vitalis (separately managed, also a Spatial Port client), plus a post office and a medical practice; shared illuminated totem and parking (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md; MASTER_PROMPT_THILAND.md). Cross-promo across the complex is treated as a non-replicable advantage (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md).
- Timeline: main services contract SP-2026-THI-001 dated 19 March 2026; discovery meetings 24 and 26 March 2026; roof of the building nearly complete April 2026; preview/Open Day Sat 24 and Sun 25 October 2026; public opening Sat 31 October 2026 (THI_LAND_Contratto_testo.txt; THI-LAND-PROJECT.md; 00-OVERVIEW-MARKETING-PLAN.md changelog 5 Aug 2026).
- One-year vision: operational venue, stable recurring-membership customer base, birthdays as acquisition engine. Five-year vision not yet explored (replicable concept vs one-shot tied to Lumino) (THI-LAND-PROJECT.md).
- Spatial Port engagement: 8 contract phases (brand identity, landing page, marketing plan, full website, CRM/access & points system via separate addendum, launch & growth 3 months, social media management 12 months, maintenance & growth 12 months); Swiss law, Canton Ticino jurisdiction (THI_LAND_Contratto_testo.txt). NXTO dashboard tracks the project as "Thi Land — Brand & Launch", active, Lumino CH (projects.ts).
